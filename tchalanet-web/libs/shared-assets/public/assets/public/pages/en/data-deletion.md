# Data Deletion

*Last updated: August 2026*

---

## Request Deletion

You may request deletion of your Tchalanet account or personal data associated with your use of Tchalanet.

To submit a request, use [Contact](/public/contact) with the subject **Data deletion** and include:

- your name;
- the organization or outlet concerned;
- the login identifier or email used;
- the associated phone number, if applicable;
- the scope of the request: account deletion, deletion of specific data, or access deactivation.

## Request Verification

To protect accounts and lottery operations, Tchalanet may verify your identity or request confirmation from the manager of the relevant organization before completing deletion.

## Data Deleted or Anonymized

After validation, Tchalanet deletes or anonymizes personal data that is no longer necessary, including account information, contact details, synchronized preferences, and support data linked to the person.

On mobile, signing out and deleting the app removes local tokens and preferences stored on the device. A server-side request is still required to delete or anonymize data already transmitted to the service.

## Data That May Be Retained

Some data may be retained when necessary to:

- comply with a legal, accounting, or regulatory obligation;
- prevent fraud or abuse;
- keep an audit history of tickets, draws, sales, scans, and controls;
- defend or exercise rights in case of a dispute;
- maintain service security and integrity.

Where possible, this data is limited, pseudonymized, or anonymized.

## Processing Time

Tchalanet processes requests within a reasonable time after receiving the information needed for verification. If the request is complex or subject to a retention obligation, Tchalanet may explain the applicable limits.
